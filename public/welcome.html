Meeqat Admin: phone app

Control your masjid screen from your Android phone. Scan the TV's QR to link it. Updates install with one tap.

⬇ Get the Admin app

iPhone: open /admin in Safari › Share › Add to Home Screen.

Install the "Downloader" app on the TV

Search for Downloader (by AFTVnews) in the TV's app store. On Fire TV: Settings › My Fire TV › Developer options › allow apps from unknown sources.

Type this-site/app

Downloader fetches Meeqat TV. Tap Install, then Open. Allow "unknown apps" for Downloader if Android asks.

Link it with your phone

The TV shows a 6-digit code and a QR. Scan it or enter the code in Phone control › Link a TV. Done: from now on the TV opens straight into your prayer screen.
